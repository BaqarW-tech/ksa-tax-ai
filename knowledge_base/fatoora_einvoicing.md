# Fatoora E-Invoicing (Phase 1 & Phase 2)

Fatoora is ZATCA's mandatory electronic invoicing system, rolled out in two phases.

## Phase 1 — Generation (since December 2021)
All VAT-registered businesses must generate invoices electronically rather than on paper,
using a compliant system that produces structured e-invoices (XML or PDF/A-3 with embedded
XML) and stores them electronically. Required fields include seller name, VAT registration
number (TRN), invoice timestamp, VAT amount, and total amount. Simplified (B2C) tax invoices
must carry a QR code.

## Phase 2 — Integration (rolling out in waves since January 2023)
Phase 2 requires businesses to integrate their e-invoicing systems directly with ZATCA's
platform (Fatoora portal), so invoices are cryptographically stamped and cleared/reported in
real time before or shortly after being issued to the customer.

ZATCA notifies affected businesses at least six months before their integration deadline.
Rollout has proceeded in successive "waves," each covering businesses above a specific annual
VAT-taxable revenue threshold, starting with the largest taxpayers and working downward.
As of **Wave 24 (announced 26 September 2025, compliance deadline 30 June 2026)**, the scope
now covers all VAT-registered businesses with annual taxable revenue above SAR 375,000 —
effectively bringing the entire VAT-registered population into Phase 2.

## Practical compliance checklist
- Confirm VAT registration and TRN display on all invoices
- Apply the correct VAT treatment (standard/zero-rated/exempt)
- Issue invoices in Arabic, with VAT shown separately
- Include QR codes on simplified B2C invoices
- File VAT returns on time (monthly if annual revenue exceeds SAR 40 million, otherwise
  quarterly), including nil returns during inactive periods
- Verify which Fatoora Phase 2 wave applies and confirm system integration before the deadline

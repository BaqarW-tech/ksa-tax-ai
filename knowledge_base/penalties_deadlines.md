# VAT Penalties and Filing Deadlines

## Filing frequency and deadlines
- **Monthly VAT returns** are required for businesses with annual revenue above SAR 40 million.
- **Quarterly VAT returns** apply to businesses at or below that threshold.
- Returns are due by the last day of the month following the end of the tax period.
- A **nil return** must still be filed even if there was no business activity in the period.

## Common penalties
- **Late registration:** a fixed penalty, typically starting at SAR 10,000, for failing to
  register within 30 days of crossing the mandatory threshold.
- **Late return submission:** fines ranging from **5% to 25% of the VAT due**, scaling with
  how late the filing is.
- **E-invoicing (Fatoora) violations:** fines for non-compliance with Phase 1 or Phase 2
  requirements, which can range roughly from SAR 5,000 up to SAR 50,000 for repeated
  violations, depending on severity.
- **Incorrect VAT charged:** applying a rate other than the correct one (e.g. charging less
  or more than 15% where standard-rated) can be reported by the public via ZATCA's channels
  and may trigger penalties on investigation.

## Disputing an assessment
If ZATCA issues a tax assessment a business believes is incorrect, there is a **60-day
window from the notification date** to file an electronic objection through the ZATCA
portal, supported by documentation and accounting or legal justification. Missing this
window forfeits the right to object, so timely professional advice is important.

## Practical takeaway
Most VAT penalties in the KSA system are avoidable through basic hygiene: registering on
time, filing every period (including nil returns), applying the correct VAT category, and
keeping e-invoicing systems current with the applicable Fatoora phase.

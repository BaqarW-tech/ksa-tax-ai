# VAT Registration in Saudi Arabia

## Thresholds
Saudi Arabia's VAT system, administered by ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority), uses a
rolling 12-month taxable-turnover test with two thresholds:

- **Mandatory registration: SAR 375,000.** Once a business's taxable supplies pass this
  amount in the last 12 months, or are expected to pass it in the next 12 months,
  registration is legally required.
- **Voluntary registration: SAR 187,500–375,000.** Businesses in this band may choose to
  register early, which lets them reclaim input VAT on purchases and build a compliance
  track record with ZATCA.
- Below SAR 187,500, VAT registration is not available.
- **Non-resident businesses** supplying taxable goods or services in the Kingdom must
  register regardless of turnover, and typically must appoint a local tax representative.

## Deadlines and process
A business generally has 30 calendar days from crossing the mandatory threshold to complete
registration on the ZATCA e-portal. The applicant needs commercial registration (CR) details,
IBAN/bank information, and revenue estimates. Once approved, ZATCA issues a 15-digit VAT
registration number (TRN) that must appear on all tax invoices.

## Deregistration
A registered business must apply for deregistration if it stops all taxable activity in the
Kingdom, or if its taxable turnover in the past 12 months falls below the voluntary threshold
of SAR 187,500.

## VAT grouping
Businesses under common ownership or control (more than 50% ownership/voting rights) may
apply for VAT group registration, treating multiple entities as a single VAT-registered unit
and eliminating VAT on intra-group transactions. Updated grouping rules took effect
15 October 2025 under Board Resolution No. (01-06-24).
